Where does alignment
research intervene?

Papers that explicitly use alignment terminology favor model-weight updates by roughly 7 to 1. Across the broader alignment literature retrieved here, the estimate drops to 1.36 to 1, with equal representation inside the 95% interval.

What changes the estimate?

Each ratio gives the estimated number of weight-update papers per external-control paper, using a stratified review. A value of 1 means equal representation. One sensitivity check also counts inference-time steering on the control side.

The primary equality test gave p = .060. The shift between periods gave p = .0023 in an exploratory comparison. All early-period mechanism observations came from 2023–24. Just five reviewed external-control papers used explicit alignment vocabulary, which accounts for the wide interval in that subset.

DPO updates the acting policy’s weights. CaMeL controls which data and tools an agent can use. SHE adapts the harness after failures. An intervention can therefore be adaptive or learned and still operate outside the acting policy.

Methods & data

The search screened 1,330,760 source records and retained 25,329 distinct candidates. A single AI reviewer coded a stratified random sample of 360. The reported ratios use that sample and its selection weights. The shortlist’s estimated held-out precision was 92% and recall was 46% within the candidate set.

The intervals capture sampling uncertainty. Reviewer judgments and omissions from retrieval add uncertainty. A venue check recovered 42 of 71 safety-titled Learning for Dynamics and Control papers. Counts measure publications; they cannot establish how funding or effort should be allocated.
