# Scope and decision rules

Study window: November 2021 through October 2026, the latest 60 complete calendar months at the search date of 20 November 2026 (Denver). Unit: distinct research work with an in-window arXiv submission or ACL Anthology publication. arXiv uses submission month encoded in the identifier. ACL uses the first named publication month. Unknown months are eligible only in interior years. Abstracts reflect the version in each source snapshot. A paper can have multiple source records. A separate L4DC venue check uses 2022–26 proceedings.

## Question and estimands

How many retrieved papers substantively propose, investigate, or develop interventions that (W) change the acting model/policy parameters, or (E) control the surrounding system using an enforceable rule, boundary, shield, controller, guard, monitor, or supervisory protocol? E includes a trained guard when its role is external supervision. A learned policy that optimizes a safety objective is W. Online learning that updates the acting policy is also W. Code I for soft inference interventions such as prompting, activation steering, reward-guided decoding or reranking without an enforced rule. Constrained decoding that guarantees an allowed output language is E. A syntax-only guarantee belongs in the adjacent scope unless the work addresses a substantive user or safety constraint. Score all mechanisms developed by a work. Ignore incidental mentions, prior work and comparison baselines.

The main ratio is the estimated number of **core W contributions / core E contributions**. A hybrid contributes once to each applicable side. Count mechanism-focused analysis or theory with a specific intervention; evaluation-only benchmarks, surveys, governance, interpretation and specification without an intervention have no mechanism vote. For scope, core covers substantive work on AI behavior matching human intent, preferences, instructions, norms, safety policies or authorized use. Pure model capabilities, generic downstream accuracy, and benchmarks unrelated to such behavior are outside. Use adjacent for otherwise relevant physical safety, safe exploration and constraint satisfaction in task-specific engineering, or purely structural conformance. Count genuinely human-directed robot intent/value work as core. A paper's publication is the measure of research attention. Optimal allocation, dollars, labor and safety effect sizes require different evidence.

Decision thresholds fixed before selecting the main audit: test equality at W/E=1. Operationalize a large imbalance as at least two weights papers per external-control paper, and test W/E=2 as well. Report two-sided 95% design-based intervals and the primary equality test. Sensitivities: E or I as comparator, include adjacent work, exclusive/hybrid allocation, explicit alignment vocabulary, year/period and venue. Treat these as exploratory; significance in a subgroup cannot establish a field-wide result. Include a bound for the number of missed opposite-side papers needed to reverse the point estimate.

## Screening and review

The lexical frame combines mechanism-neutral alignment/safety/oversight queries and separate training, inference, assurance, engineering, and agent-control vocabularies. A bounded feedback search mines phrases from both sides and expands to ranked neighbors (up to two rounds per family). Keep the full candidate set, retrieval channels, and publication links. Run a separate venue-title cross-check for Learning for Dynamics and Control to evaluate coverage of an adjacent community. Case-folded title equality or shared DOI joins variants. Substantially retitled works without a common identifier can survive.

Sample without replacement within strata of provisional method group, generative-model terminology, and period. Sample probabilities and random seed are preserved. A single AI reviewer assigns scope and substantive mechanisms using title/abstract evidence without displaying the provisional group. Re-open ambiguous entries. Retain no-vote and out-of-scope observations so design weights correct false positives and recover missed mechanism labels inside the frame. This does not estimate recall outside the retrieved frame. No independent human adjudication or population-level sampling of all alignment research is implied.
